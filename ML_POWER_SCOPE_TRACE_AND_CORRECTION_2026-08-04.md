## Layman result

The literal phrase **“material power” was not in any ML* Git version or in the Audit 55 ZIP**. It was introduced as shorthand in the August 4 conversation.

The ZIP did already contain the older wording **“power’s material dominance.”** Its proper job was narrow: it was condition A of a two-condition test for deciding whether a circulating phrase counts as a hivemindidiom. It was not a general rule for ranking which gorgonifications deserve attention.

The twist happened when that old phrase-classifier was promoted into a general project-priority test. That promotion was new and unsupported. The current correction preserves power analysis but removes the material-only narrowing and blocks the classifier from taking over attention triage.

Current condition A: a phrase’s circulation serves the reproduction of a **specific power-formation**.  
Current condition B: the phrase operates as **prepackaged not-thinking below the critical layer**.  
Both remain required.

Material effects can still be traced inside a particular gorgonification spiral as a substrate, output, or reinforcement. They are not a required or sufficient definition, and they do not decide what deserves attention. Attention triage routes to sequential engagement and the Sabachtan internal-logic killswitch: first ask whether the position follows its own logic.
