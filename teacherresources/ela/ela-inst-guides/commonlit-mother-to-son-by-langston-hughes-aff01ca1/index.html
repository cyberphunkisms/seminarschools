Classroom fit

CommonLit module with full text, guided reading questions, discussion questions. Direct paired-poem resource for Thank You M’am: Mrs. Jones as the mother-figure delivering hard-earned wisdom to a young person. In the fictional 1958 Harlem: a bookworm sitting on the stoop of a boarding house hears an older woman reciting these words to her son. Free with educator account.
