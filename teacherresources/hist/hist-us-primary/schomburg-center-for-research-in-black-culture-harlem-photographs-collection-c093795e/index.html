Digital collection of Harlem photographs. The campaign page already uses a ca. 1946 photograph of West 125th Street from this collection. For bookwormburrows: these photographs are the visual substrate of the dimension. Teachers can project them as the DM describes the street the bookworms are walking. Free, digitized.
