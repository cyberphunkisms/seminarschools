# Rhetoric Taxonomy Conversation Ledger

Date: 2026-07-29  
Source status: direct conversation rulings, provisional research, and explicit deferrals  
Scope: ML* rhetoric taxonomy only  

This ledger preserves the distinctions reached in the conversation without converting an unfinished taxonomy into a finished tree. The Fool monograph remains deferred.

## Status key

- SETTLED: directly corrected or accepted by the author and safe to use now.
- PROVISIONAL: useful candidate or working discriminator that still requires dialectical testing.
- DEFERRED: preserved for later work and barred from current expansion.
- EXCLUDED: rejected routing or classification.

## Settled architecture

1. Use a multi-level matrix. One event can occupy several levels at once.
2. Keep at least these levels separate:
   - utterance or rhetorical form
   - tactic or move
   - operator or recurring governing role
   - governing end
   - audience relation and intended audience endpoint
   - performance contract
   - awareness
   - truth relation
   - commodified object
   - reward or function
   - authority and sanction
   - coordination or apparatus
   - state, process, transition, and system effect
3. Use family-to-subfamily inheritance only where the child actually inherits the parent operation. Use axes, tags, and hybrid labels for cross-cutting relations.
4. Rhetoric is the morally open root. Sophism, Jesterism, Sirenism, trolling, pedagogy, counsel, mobilization, and coercive boundary cases are possible routes through rhetoric.
5. A sophistical move and a Sophist actor occupy different levels. A person can repeat a sophistical form without being organized around Sophistry.
6. Kayfabe is an orthogonal performance contract. Jesters, Trolls, Sirens, Demagogues, Gorgons, entertainers, and other operators can all enter a kayfabe relation.
7. Marketing is a reward or function layer. Attention, status, reach, sales, votes, loyalty, and authority belong on this axis.
8. Gorgonification is a process. Gorgon is a reversible actor-state. Gorgonlogic is automatic reasoning-layer discharge. None of these should be flattened into an intentional rhetoric-operator family.
9. Snakelogic already names a mechanism in ML*. Create no redundant rhetoric family called Snakelogic.

## Settled family and operator rulings

### Jester, Fool, and Trickster

- Trickster belongs inside the Jester/Fool family.
- The family can contain different narrative, ritual, comic, literary, and rhetorical variants.
- Comic license alone establishes a form or position. It supplies no automatic ethical certification.
- The counsel-bearing Jester spends the bit, persona, safety, status, or access so difficult counsel can pass.
- The controlling truth-sacrifice test is: the persona or bit is sacrificed before truth.
- Cancellation, suffering, obscurity, or self-destruction alone do not prove Jesterism. Martyr theater remains possible.

### Parrhesiast

- Parrhesiast remains a useful category.
- Parrhesia is direct, answerable, risk-bearing frank speech.
- Jester and Parrhesiast can overlap. Jester routes counsel through humor, folly, obscenity, paradox, or liminal license. Parrhesiast says it directly and accepts the danger of answerability.
- The Sabachtan Gnostic as both remains a plausible overlap, not a completed taxonomy ruling.

### Troll

- Troll is its own operator category.
- Trolling is the tactic. Troll is a recurring role organized around producing reaction, antagonism, confusion, disruption, threshold pressure, or spectator amusement.
- A Troll can expose a real contradiction or become abusive. Provocation supplies no automatic truth and no automatic malice.
- Troll remains separate from Eristic Sophism. A Troll may use sophistical moves without becoming a Sophist subtype.

### Sophist

- The Sophist governs the trick. The Gorgon is governed by it. Gorgonification makes it reproduce.
- The controlling distinction already present in ML* is verbal facility without answerable commitment.
- A Sophist actor attribution requires a pattern of governing, changing, or preserving moves for payoff or correction-immunity. A single fallacy or mistake establishes only a move.

### Siren

- Siren is a required operator category. Its complete hierarchy remains open.
- Siren is strategic rather than merely repetitive. A single utterance cannot establish that awareness.
- Siren uses attraction, attention, trust, desire, moral authority, or apparent intimacy to preserve dependency or extract value.
- For this project the Siren is grounded in prostitution or conversion into a storefront. Body, morality, empathy, intimacy, suffering, knowledge, rebellion, identity, and causes can become saleable assets.
- Instant moral certainty is one possible song. It is not the essence of Siren.
- Panderer and Flatterer route into Sirenism because gratification is exchanged for favor, loyalty, or retention.
- Siren can use a true statement instrumentally. Falsehood is not required.
- Siren awareness requires longitudinal evidence such as repeated deployment, benefits, correction behavior, audience dependency, production evidence, or strategic adaptation.
- The Siren sacrifices truth, audience independence, or the object of concern to preserve the persona, market, authority, or dependency.
- Ancient mythic Siren, rhetorical Siren operator, and Gorgonwars Siren character or faction are distinct registers. The Gorgonwars Gorgon-Siren-Witch boundary remains unresolved.

### Wizard

- Wizard or Magic already names morally open conscious deployment of an idiom or framing device.
- Wizard and Siren cannot be separated by awareness alone.
- The Wizard discriminator is the listener's subjectivity trajectory. Pedagogical black magic has an eventual exit. It helps the listener outgrow the scaffold.
- Siren preserves dependency or extraction. A Wizard can become manipulative when that exit disappears.

## Baselinemorality and prostitutionism

### Settled four-stage chain

1. Commodification converts a relationship, value, capacity, or obligation into something exchangeable.
2. Legitimation or moralization explains why that conversion is proper, righteous, necessary, liberating, natural, inevitable, expert-approved, or legally authorized.
3. Normalization makes the conversion ordinary, default, and taken for granted.
4. Enforcement makes refusal, dissent, or nonparticipation costly.

Baselinemorality requires this full moral-economic chain. Commodifiedempathy supplies the market logic. Emotional labor prostitution converts mutual obligation into priced service. Baselinemorality supplies calculated moral currency. Justification and normalization cut deeper than mere commodification because they turn the market relation into a moral duty and can make objection appear unethical.

Virtue signalling can describe the visible status display. It misses the deeper operation when moral concern itself becomes a commodity, produces jurisdiction, legitimates the exchange, normalizes it, and preserves dependency.

### Provisional coinages and placements

- Prostitutionism: the ideology that justifies, moralizes, normalizes, and reproduces commodification.
- Moralized commodification: descriptive name for the mechanism.
- Prostitutionist Siren: proposed Siren branch whose song sells and morally legitimates the sale.
- Ticktockdancing Siren: proposed surface-performance subtype. The medium and dance do not define the governing operator.

These names remain provisional until the author ratifies definitions and relations.

## Provisional operator and axis candidates

### Highest-value corruptive or extractive candidates

1. Legitimator or Consecrator
   - Function: makes a contingent practice or order seem morally right, necessary, natural, inevitable, expert-approved, or authorized.
   - Collision to test: operator family versus normative-function axis.
   - Strong relation: Baselinemorality.

2. Indoctrinator
   - Function: installs doctrine while suppressing critical examination, alternatives, and exit.
   - Collision to test: corrupted Wizard subtype versus audience-closure axis.

3. Rule Enforcer or Inquisitor
   - Function: controls eligibility, access, recantation, confession, and punishment.
   - Collision to test: conscious sanctioning operator versus automatic Gorgonification.

4. Recruiter or Proselytizer
   - Function: succeeds when the audience becomes another carrier or recruiter.
   - Collision to test: operator family versus reproduction or conversion endpoint.

5. Exhorter, Mobilizer, Agitator, or Inciter
   - Function: turns existing judgment, grievance, or contradiction into action.
   - Collision to test: neutral mobilization axis versus evidence-sacrificing operator.

6. Agent Provocateur
   - Function: induces rash, illegal, violent, or discrediting action so the reaction becomes evidence or pretext against the target.
   - Difference from Troll: the provoked reaction is weaponized as justification for repression or discrediting.

7. Moral Crusader or Rule Creator
   - Function: defines an evil and creates rules to eliminate it.
   - Collision to test: Legitimator, Demagogue, and Moral Entrepreneur.

8. Accuser or Kategoria
   - Function: organizes public judgment around a charge and demanded answer.
   - Collision to test: forensic scene role versus operator family.

9. Apologist or Image Repairer
   - Function: repairs legitimacy, reputation, or jurisdiction after challenge.
   - Collision to test: rhetorical scene role versus Legitimator subtype.

10. Gatekeeper
    - Function: controls entry, visibility, recognition, or eligibility.
    - Collision to test: authority position versus rhetorical operator.

11. Confidence Operator or Con Artist
    - Function: converts trust into a bounded extraction.
    - Difference to test: one-shot or terminal extraction versus Siren's preserved dependency.

12. Functionary, Careerist, or Classical Hypocrite
    - Function: repeats or administers a line for advantage without requiring belief in it.
    - Collision to test: Kenocharism's Classical Hypocrite, Operator, unresolved Nimrod, and Gorgon state.

13. Coercer or Threatener
    - Function: secures compliance by making refusal costly.
    - Status: boundary of rhetoric and force rather than persuasion proper.

14. False-authority cluster
    - Quack: sincerely mistakes incompetence for competence.
    - Charlatan: knows the claimed competence is false.
    - Predatory expert: possesses competence and abuses the authority it grants.
    - Status: awareness and authority modifiers rather than one mythic family.

### Highest-value constructive or audience-liberating candidates

15. Maieutic Midwife
    - Function: helps another person generate and test their own understanding.
    - Success condition: the facilitator becomes dispensable.
    - Existing ML* anchor: CL-51 AI as maieutic extractor.

16. Deliberative Counselor
    - Function: offers answerable counsel about future action, tradeoffs, and consequences.
    - Relation: Jester and Parrhesiast are special risk and register modes.

17. Mediator
    - Function: helps parties pursue a party-controlled settlement.
    - Difference from Dialectician: settlement of conflict rather than transformation of concepts.

18. Witness or Testifier
    - Function: preserves experience in public memory and demands recognition.
    - Overlap: high-risk witnessing can become Parrhesia.

19. Rhetorical Listener
    - Function: receives speech to understand its actual logic and remain corrigible.
    - Dark use: Siren can listen instrumentally to locate vulnerability.
    - Likely placement: audience-relation axis.

20. Relay, Messenger, or Amplifier
    - Function: carries and scales a message.
    - Likely placement: transmission role rather than governing operator.

### Apparatus and scene candidates

- Propaganda belongs primarily on the coordination and apparatus axis.
- Demagoguery belongs primarily to political in-group and out-group mobilization.
- Outrage marketing belongs on the reward and function axis.
- Authority source belongs on an axis with charismatic, traditional, and rational-legal modes.
- Rhetorical scene belongs on an axis with forensic, deliberative, and epideictic modes.

## Collision tests

1. Reward substitution: change money to status, votes, attention, or loyalty while preserving conduct. A changing label was probably a reward category.
2. Medium removal: remove the platform, occupation, dance, or genre. A vanishing label was probably a surface form.
3. Exit test: give the audience real independence and the ability to outgrow the speaker. Midwife and pedagogical Wizard release. Siren and Indoctrinator resist.
4. Authority removal: remove institutional sanction. A vanishing operation was likely Enforcer or Gatekeeper.
5. Coordination removal: remove organization and message discipline. A vanishing classification was probably Propaganda as apparatus.
6. Awareness substitution: change strategic awareness to sincere repetition. This separates Siren, Charlatan, and conscious Operator from Gorgon and Quack.
7. Truth substitution: keep the governing end and change the statement from false to true. Siren, Troll, Propagandist, and Sophist may still operate through true material.
8. Audience endpoint:
   - author of an insight: Midwife
   - better reasoner: Dialectician
   - counseled decision-maker: Counselor
   - warned witness: Parrhesiast
   - dependent consumer: Siren
   - reactor: Troll
   - obedient subject: Indoctrinator or Enforcer
   - active partisan: Exhorter or Demagogue
   - new carrier: Recruiter
   - script repeater: Gorgon
9. Truth-sacrifice test: what is sacrificed first under pressure, the bit and persona, or truth and audience independence?
10. Longitudinal evidence test: actor categories require patterns, benefits, adaptation, correction behavior, and audience relations. A single utterance establishes a form or possible move.
