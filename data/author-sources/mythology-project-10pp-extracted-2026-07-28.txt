Two years ago, you already had the monster, the snakes, the mirror, the cave, the desert, the war, and the
intuition that mythology could carry philosophy where direct exposition failed. What the project lacked
was an apparatus capable of preserving those discoveries against simplification, assistant invention, category
drift, false synthesis, and the endless repetition of earlier errors.

The progress is qualitative. #Gorgonwars has moved from an explosive mythology-building conversation
into one application inside a complete philosophical, exegetical, pedagogical, citational, and
publication architecture.

   ●​ Gorgons speak through snakes.

The old conversation also shows why the later methodology became necessary. You repeatedly supplied a
precise distinction, the assistant generalized it, and you then had to restore the particular. The assistant

In the old conversation, mythology operated through intuition. You could recognize that the Force, qi, religious

The current framework distinguishes:

   ●​   coherence inside a world
   ●​   correspondence to an external referent
   ●​   compatibility across worlds or traditions
   ●​   fuzzy membership without identity
   ●​   analogy from genealogy
   ●​   convergence from transmission
   ●​   symbolic relation from historical attribution
   ●​   source-supported claims from authorial synthesis

The r/topcharactertropes material now supplies an always-already example. Participants test whether a
character fits the internal rules of the story and the external trope being named, usually without theoretical
vocabulary. Your current two-gate rule retains candidates that satisfy both kinds of logic before any
character-smashing or synthesis begins. Mezo logic can now occupy the cross-world compatibility problem
between those gates rather than functioning as an unrestricted resemblance machine.

Two years ago, you were already doing the operation. The recent work has made the operation inspectable.

7. The framework can now lose
The old Gorgon vocabulary carried a genuine danger. A critic could be called Gorgonic, and that diagnosis
could become self-sealing. The framework could turn every objection into proof of itself.

The recent work built exact controls against that outcome:

    ●​   A diagnosis requires inspectable evidence.
    ●​   Another participant must be able to retrace, contest, and alter it.
    ●​   Criticism may exclude a claim.
    ●​   A Point may divide, dissolve, or be replaced.
    ●​   A relation may be removed.
    ●​   The Stone may fracture.
    ●​   An application may produce several Stones or none.
    ●​   The model may be abandoned.
    ●​   Naming a criticism never automatically converts it into another Stone side.
    ●​   Repetition never certifies the audit.

The all-perspectives audits explicitly test whether the framework absorbs criticism, hides behind disclaimers,
turns later drafts into authority, or confuses a beautiful figure with evidentiary proof.

Two years ago, the project attacked self-sealing ideologies. Today, it has procedures preventing itself from
becoming one.
