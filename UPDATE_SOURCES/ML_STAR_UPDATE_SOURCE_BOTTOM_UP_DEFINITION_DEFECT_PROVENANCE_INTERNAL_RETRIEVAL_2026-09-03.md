## 2. Defect provenance before substantive classification

### Direct user corrections

> “remember the critic is not authority, seperate the substantive issues it brings up from the rheotircal issues that are your fault for not ironmanning well.”

> “dont take the critic as an authority, keep everything we worked on as is, just use it to help improve the next version unlesss there are substanial issues, then stop and tell me what they are”

Existing ML* already treated criticism as objection rather than authority and required ironmanning. The remaining failure was reporting an objection to an assistant-authored sentence, bridge, or paraphrase as a defect in the user’s thesis.

### Adopted operational form

Before reporting an objection as a substantive problem in the user’s work or project, trace the attacked claim to its author. If the objection defeats only an assistant-authored paraphrase, bridge, premise, or extension, classify it as the assistant’s rhetorical or interpretive defect and remove it from the substantive-objection list. A substantive objection survives only when it reaches a user-authored or adopted claim in its strongest actual form.

This rule does not immunize the user’s work from criticism. It assigns the defect to the proposition and author actually defeated.
