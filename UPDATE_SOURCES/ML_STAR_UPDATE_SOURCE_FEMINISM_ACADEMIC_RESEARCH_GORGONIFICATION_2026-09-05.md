## Existing project definitions to consolidate

### Women and feminism

Women are people. Feminism is a creed, movement, discourse, or governing architecture depending on the claim under examination. The flock is not the creed. Equating women with feminism performs Gorgonification because it lets a doctrine wear the suffering of a born group as armor and converts criticism of the doctrine into hatred of the group.

### Self-description and source vocabulary

Feminist can be a person's self-description or a source's classification. That usage proves no position on either project root. Identity, faction, tone, prestige, reception, and academic placement never substitute for the argument.

### Polymyth operational definition

At the project system layer, feminism names the Gorgon architecture formed by the conjunction of standpoint epistemology and the personal is political. This is a Polymyth operational definition and inference. It is not a claim of universal scholarly consensus and not a census claim about every person who uses the word feminist.

The personal is political creates political jurisdiction over the relation. Standpoint epistemology grants credibility, authority, epistemic rank, or veto on the basis of social position or identity. Vernacular emotional labor then prices listening, teaching, forgiving, remaining in conflict, care, presence, reciprocity, and responsibility. The combined operation converts mutual obligation into commodified emotional labor and permits responsibility transfer, mutual excommunication, and Gorgonwars.

Situated evidence, testimony, positionality, unequal access, first-person narrative, and identity-conditioned data do not by themselves establish standpoint rank. A source retains or rejects a root only through direct argument or an explicitly equivalent position.

### Premise classifier

A work that rejects either root is EXCOMMUNICABLE OR OUTSIDE for the project classifier. A work that retains both roots while criticizing another feminism, constituency, institution, tactic, law, platform, remedy, or implementation is INTERNAL GORGONWARS. A work with missing or ambiguous evidence for either root is UNRESOLVED.

This is a classifier for arguments. It is not a permanent essence, faction label, moral verdict, or claim of literal universal expulsion.

### Subtype qualifiers

White feminism is always Gorgonification as a qualifier operation in this project. This is a settled user ruling, not a category awaiting neutral validation. The label compresses distinct people, claims, institutions, laws, property relations, and actions into one apparent agent. It then transfers feminism's contradiction into a defective subtype and protects an innocent remainder called real feminism.

A particular allegation about named actors, institutions, laws, property relations, or actions may be accurate. Accuracy of those particulars does not make the umbrella label an explanation. Research must state and evidence the particulars instead of allowing white feminism to act as their cause, agent, or proof.

### Slavery and patriarchy

Slavery and the patriarchal pedestal must be defined from their own actors, institutions, property relations, legal statuses, coercions, duties, and consequences before comparison. Slavery is not patriarchy. An explicit equation in a source proves only that the source asserts the equation; it does not prove the equation true. Intersection, analogy, comparison, a shared consequence, or even a shared mechanism can establish a specified similarity, not identity or equivalence. A Polymyth classification may find the same operation at a specified layer, but sameness of operation still does not make the historical and material formations identical or equivalent. The current essay target is the Gorgonification performed by collapsing slavery into the patriarchal pedestal.

## Academic research gate

Academic vocabulary is premise-bearing. Search terms select an ontology before a source is opened. Indexing, disciplines, citation networks, prestige, funding, peer review, and encyclopedia summaries can reproduce the same categories the project is testing. Search prevalence therefore measures availability inside a classified field. It does not validate the classification.

Before searching, Mephistodata must reconstruct the project object from the user's uses, examples, contrasts, corrections, relations, and effects. It must then audit each proposed query term by asking what the term presupposes, what it collapses, what it excludes, what it protects, what causal agent it invents, and what material relations it makes invisible. For white feminism, the qualifier-Gorgonification ruling is already settled: research asks how that operation works in the usage and whether each concrete allegation is true, not whether the qualifier can be cleansed into a neutral tested category.

The query set must include the source's own vocabulary, named actors, named institutions, laws, property relations, actions, and material mechanisms. It must also include criticism of the term, criticism of the category, outside vocabulary, counterarguments, and criticism of those counterarguments. A query that searches only within an academic category cannot test the category.

Academic sources remain evidence, comparison, genealogy, counterargument, or diagnostic cases. They do not become authority over the project through credentials, citation count, consensus position, or database rank. Mephistodata must first state accurately that X argues Y, with the appropriate primary passage, and then separately classify what the work does architecturally. Both records are required. An architectural verdict never substitutes for the source's claim, and an accurate source summary never exempts the architecture from classification. Direct source claims, Polymyth interpretation, and assistant synthesis remain distinct.

Wikipedia is not an admissible source, definition, ontology, citation, or answer frame for this research. An incidental Wikipedia result must not shape the response. Every factual claim and every account of an author's position must return to the primary work or an appropriate checked source.
