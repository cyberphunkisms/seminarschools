## What the source supports

- Academic credentials, chairs, curricula, research permissions, peer bodies, disciplinary titles, canons, and career structures can become instruments of intellectual jurisdiction when they determine who owns an object or which questions may be asked. (Detienne, Foreword and pp. 9–24, 36–38, 57–65.)
- This is a critique of credentialed jurisdiction rather than a blanket rejection of scholarship. Detienne's constructive method depends on philology, fieldwork, archaeology, archives, languages, local reconstruction, and sustained specialist competence. (pp. 19–24, 60–78.)
- Comparables are built through declared questions and relationally reconstructed cases. Distant and resistant cases can expose hidden assumptions in familiar categories. (pp. ix–xv, 22–32.)
- Boundaries have sources, forms, enforcers, permeability regimes, positions, membership effects, material consequences, and histories. (pp. 26–35, 72–77, 79–99.)
- Equality inside an assembly does not determine who may enter it. Spatial design, speech procedure, publicity, decision rules, and enforcement participate in producing the political form. (pp. 79–99.)
- The Greek divine figures in the worked analysis are positional and plural within constraints. This supports a bounded relational comparison method. It does not prove a universal thesis about every figure in every tradition. (pp. 59–78.)

## Opposition must survive an adequate test

Ironmanning does not mean forcing an adverse verdict. An adverse relation is established only when the strongest actual project claim and the strongest relevant source claim are reconstructed at matching scope and the source supplies a real reason against that claim.

The current relation vocabulary permits strict contradiction, substantive incompatibility, partial opposition, negative evidence under an observable and adequate test, and a failed adequate test. It also permits full or scoped support, compatible refinement, neutral broadening, neutral partial overlap, neutral difference, noncoverage, and non-opposition-as-presented. The label follows the evidence; the label cannot create the evidence.

A documented historical relay can defeat a scoped independence claim. It cannot automatically make the whole source adverse to the whole project. A full positive test can support the tested target while leaving extra candidate cases outside the target. Partly overlapping sets are not forced into opposition, refinement, or broadening when neither set contains the other.

## Executable fixture coverage

The executable adjudication fixtures are designed to make the distinction enforceable rather than rhetorical.

- A relation cannot support itself by using the same claim as target and candidate.
- `failed_adequate_test` and `negative_evidence` require an adequate test with the facts needed for that result; a bare relation label cannot manufacture them.
- `nonopposition_as_presented` is restricted to the provenance route it describes and cannot masquerade as source support or user adoption.
- Strict contradiction and substantive incompatibility operate at the whole current-claim scope; partial opposition operates at part-of-current-claim scope.
- Evidence-aware rendering distinguishes what a source supports from what a test supports.
- Equal allowed sets, proper subsets, proper supersets, disjoint sets, and partial overlaps receive distinct relations; overlapping sets cannot be mislabeled merely because each contains something the other lacks.
