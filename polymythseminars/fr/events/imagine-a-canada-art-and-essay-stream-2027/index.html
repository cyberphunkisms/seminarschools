À propos de cette fiche

The National Centre for Truth and Reconciliation runs Imagine a Canada as a recurring program for Kindergarten through Grade 12 and CEGEP students. Grade 4 students may submit an essay or another creative representation of a reconciled Canada. The organizer has not announced a 2027 edition, deadline, fee, or word limit. January 1 serves as a calendar watch marker for the next cycle.
