## Controlled-archive evidence method

The method determines the archive condition before interpreting a null result. It records access, selection, visibility, deletion, ranking, publication, chilling, departure, non-entry, and retained-population effects. It distinguishes the observable record from the process that produced it.

Five evidence-condition flags remain separate:

- `OPEN`: the relevant channel is materially observable.
- `RESTRICTED`: access or visibility is bounded.
- `SELECTED`: inclusion depends on a non-neutral filter.
- `CHILLED`: expected records may never be produced.
- `UNKNOWN`: the archive condition cannot yet be established.

Documented obstruction can support a claim about obstruction. It does not recover the exact missing content. A clean unsupported status applies when the proposition lacks support and the record lacks an adequate obstruction mechanism. Metrics produced after exclusion are treated as endogenous to the institution that created the retained population. Harm classification and intervention remain separate decisions.
