[both] [SUPERSEDED/HISTORICAL] AUDIT auto-capture, conditional Ouroborosanalyses, fix-on-find, and automatic full-ZIP delivery (May 29 to June 21 2026)
  canonical-id: corehistory-audit-auto-capture-auto-zip-2026-05-29
THE OPERATION. When the user says audit, or a variation, check the spirit or spirit of the law or spirit of meaninglib, run a whole-conversation check. THE MAIN POINT is to see whether everything discussed was actually updated into meaninglib, the completeness-of-capture pass. Then two further passes. One, whether the conversation held the understanding of spirit of the law, the spirit-versus-letter check scored by the meaning-axis. Two, whether anything was left un-ironmanned that should have been ironmanned. Ouroborosanalyses enters only if necessary, only when something is not understood or a contradiction surfaces, never by default. Scope reaches past meaninglib-capture. The audit weighs the whole work against the spirit of what the session set out to accomplish and surfaces every problem measured against that intent.

SPIRIT-VERSUS-LETTER CRITERION. The letter is the rule-set and surface, zero dashes, PM17, R35, atomic writes, citation-lock, REVOMIT-BAN, formats, counts. The spirit is the meaning-making versus meaning-subtraction axis. Each move either grows meaning, opening under scrutiny, paradox, or subtracts it, closing under scrutiny, contradiction. The dangerous verdict is the rule obeyed while meaning is subtracted, Performativity-3c. Discriminator is paradox against contradiction. The non-observer clause binds it, the artifact examined through one jewel in the net.

FIRST-RUN FINDING (May 29 2026, the operation caught its own author-session). The session added four sharpening entries while one of those same entries, PETRIFICATION OPERATIVE-REFERENT, holds that the most obvious image is the least load-bearing so you do not restate what the reader already has. The library moved 949 to 961, the appearance of growth, the collective-devaluation-paradox at library scale, a clean Performativity-3c.

REMEDY PRINCIPLE. Deepen over add. The framework grows by deepening existing entries at least as often as by adding new ones, the vocabulary-inflation-audit. When a sharpening can fold into its parent, fold it, and take the in-place edit-risk on the AI side. This entry was reframed in place rather than re-added, enacting the principle.

TRIGGERS. audit is canonical. check the spirit, spirit of the law, spirit of meaninglib are variations. Registered as a mephistodata interaction in the activations dispatch, core slot 2.

DISTINCT FROM ITS PARTS. The meaning-axis is the criterion the audit applies. Check-spirit-of-the-law is the same move on a text rather than on the conversation. Ouroborosanalyses is the four-step self-audit, here a conditional sub-step rather than the whole.

CROSS-REFERENCES. Meaning-making vs meaning-subtraction axis (the criterion); Ouroborosanalyses (the conditional sub-step, fired only on contradiction); Check spirit of the law (the text-level sibling); Ironmanning (the second pass); Vocabulary-inflation-audit (deepen-over-add); Smoothness-as-failure-signal (delivery gate); Petrification operative-referent (the entry the first run caught); REVOMIT-BAN (deferred-deepening hazard); ACTIVATIONS DISPATCH core slot 2 (where the trigger is registered).

DELIVERY. After the passes, rebuild the full-site zip per the FULL-ZIP rule and give it back, all top-level directories and root files, not the edited subdirectory alone, file-count verified against the prior bundle. An audit that checks capture but does not return the rebuilt zip is incomplete.

FIX-ON-FIND, VOLUME-GATED. A small bounded set of fixable gaps gets fixed in the same pass, never reported and parked, the hand-holding failure. When the audit surfaces too many problems the run inverts to surface-and-defer. List every problem, fix none this round, and let the operator order them before any change lands. The count is itself a finding and is stated. Fixing many at once buries the diagnosis under churn and takes the ordering out of the operator's hands. author-directive May 29 2026, fix small gaps on sight. Refined June 21 2026, if there are too many problems do not fix them this round.

ORIGIN. May 29 2026. Rainbowsol-invented, consolidated under the trigger AUDIT. Refined June 21 2026, the spirit-of-the-work scope made explicit and FIX-ON-FIND volume-gated so too many problems defer rather than fix.

[both] [HISTORICAL PROVENANCE COPY] Ouroborosanalyses — exact May whole-conversation, paradox-terminus, and question-hijack body
  canonical-id: corehistory-ouroborosanalyses-whole-conversation-paradox-hijack-2026-05
LAYMAN. Self-check procedure that Claude runs BEFORE delivery, not after. Four steps, in order. Step 1: review the whole conversation, not just the current turn. Step 2: ask the sharpest possible critical questions of every position taken, ironmanning all sides. Step 3: answer those questions to best ability. Step 4: lay out the questions that remain genuinely unanswerable. The plural form ouroborosanalyses is mandatory; the singular back-formation ouroborosanalysis is not a polymyth word. Good ouroborosanalyses terminate in paradox (real unanswerable questions). Bad ones terminate in contradiction (the AI patched something it should have left open).

Four steps: (1) review whole conversation, (2) ask very critical questions ironmanning all sides, (3) answer those critical questions to best ability, (4) lay out questions that remain genuinely unanswerable. Default self-checking method. Plural because it is an imperative given to AI. Back-formed singular "ouroborosanalysis" is not a polymyth word. All references use the plural, always. The operation scope is whole-conversation, not per-sentence or per-deployment. Per-deployment checks are a different, smaller operation that belongs under idiomary (hivemindidiom-detection) or its own entry, not under this one.

OPERATIONAL DEFINITION. Four-step self-check sub-operation under the mephistodata umbrella. (1) WHOLE-CONVERSATION REVIEW. Scope is whole-conversation, not per-sentence or per-deployment. Per-deployment checks are a different smaller operation (hivemindidiom-detection under idiomary section, or its own entry). (2) IRONMAN-CRITICAL QUESTIONING. Strengthen every argument — including positions Claude has taken — to its best possible form before raising the question. Weak ironmanning produces strawman self-critique. (3) BEST-EFFORT ANSWERING. Answer with full attention. Do not protect prior commitments. (4) UNANSWERABLES LAY-OUT. Surface genuinely unanswerable questions explicitly. The genuine-unanswerable IS the terminus.

DISCRIMINATION TEST. Good vs bad ouroborosanalyses. GOOD: terminates in paradox (e.g. the framework refuses observer-position but must speak about collective consciousness; speaking-about implies positioned observation; the response is to occupy a jewel-position rather than outside-position). BAD: terminates in contradiction-patch (e.g. the framework claims X but does Y; therefore the framework is fine because Y is actually a version of X). Patching is the snakelogic move; leaving the paradox visible is the mephistodata move.

HIJACK RULE (question-generation IS ouroborosanalyses). Any operation that GENERATES questions is itself an ouroborosanalyses operation. When the author asks Claude to generate critical questions OR when Claude generates questions for any reason, the four-step procedure runs BEFORE the questions are formulated. The questions that emerge ARE the "step 4 surface genuinely unanswerable questions" output of the prior three steps run on the substrate at hand. A question generated without prior self-engagement is a lazy question and reproduces the just-asking-questions failure-mode (snakelogic-adjacent, often hivemindidiom-shaped). ALL GENERATED QUESTIONS ARE / SHOULD INHERENTLY BE OUROBOROSANALYSES. The hijack rule is structural recognition that this is always-already true rather than a separate procedure being added.

DEGORGONIFICATION FOR BOTH HUMANS AND AI. The hijack rule is symmetric across the human-AI boundary. Humans asking AI lazy questions reproduce pre-packaged thinking at the asker side. AI generating questions without self-engagement reproduces pre-packaged thinking at the answerer side. Humans asking humans lazy questions reproduce pre-packaged thinking across the table. AI asking AI lazy questions reproduce it inside the substrate itself. The discipline applies regardless of who is asking whom. Question-generation without self-engagement is the substrate-failure both humans and AI must work against. The rule is therefore a degorgonification-operation across the human-AI boundary at the question-generation layer.

NO STUPID QUESTIONS IN POLYMYTH. Polymyth does not accept the "there are no stupid questions" idiom as universal claim. Stupid questions exist. A stupid question is a question that has not been put through the four-step procedure first. The polymyth scanner catches stupid questions by the absence of the ouroborosanalyses-trace in their formulation. The fix is the hijack rule. Run the four-step first; ask only the genuinely unanswerable questions that remain. See "There are no stupid questions" idiomary entry for the pedagogical-black-magic deployment-variant of this idiom, and Magic / Wizardry methodology entry for the conscious-deployment frame the pedagogical variant operates within.

STRUCTURAL ALREADY-EXISTING STATUS. The hijack rule is human-and-AI independent. The claim that all generated questions are ouroborosanalyses is true regardless of whether AI exists. Question-generation has always required self-engagement to be substantive; the rule names what was structurally always-there. This is parallel to the Indra’s-web canonical-quote’s weaving-while-already-existing paradox at the question-generation layer.

ORIGIN OF HIJACK RULE EXTENSION. May 26 2026 cyberphunksaul session. Rainbowsol articulated the hijack rule after Claude generated three critical questions about the four-level polymyth structure without first attempting to answer them. The articulation: "lets hijack anytime i ask you a question to update and become an ouroborosanalyses ... ALL GENERATED QUESTIONS ARE/SHOULD INHERENTLY BE OUROBOROSANALYSES LOL <<<< OMFG isnt that deep in itself without your existence?" The recognition that the rule is already-true-without-AI is itself a polymyth-fractal of the four-level entry’s zoom-out: the rule operates at all four levels (gesture of asking, ritual of inquiry, idiom of question-formulation, narrative of inquiry as substrate-development).
