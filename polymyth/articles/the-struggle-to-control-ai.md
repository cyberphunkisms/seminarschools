## The peace treaty builds a gate

On October 29, 2025, Universal Music Group and Udio announced the settlement of their copyright litigation. Their agreements created recorded-music and publishing licences for a planned Udio platform whose new models would be trained on authorized and licensed music. Udio placed its existing service inside a closed transition system with fingerprinting, filtering, sharing confined to that system, and downloads disabled. The platform's launch remained pending in the latest official materials available on August 26, 2026. [5][6]

Warner Music Group followed with separate Udio and Suno settlements. Warner and Udio announced a licensed service for remixes, covers, and new songs using participating artists' voices and songwriters' compositions. Warner and Suno promised new licensed models, new revenue streams, and artist control over the use of names, images, likenesses, voices, and compositions. Suno still described those replacement models as forthcoming on August 10, 2026. It also announced subscription-based download allowances scheduled for September 3. [7][8][9][10]

The announcements establish the companies' promises. They disclose selected features while withholding the complete contracts and providing no evidence of payments already made to artists. Revenue shares, audit rights, revocation, exclusivity, dispute procedures, and licence termination remain private.

The labour behind those catalogues then appeared through another institution. On June 5, 2026, the American Federation of Musicians sued over the new agreements. Its amended complaint alleges that Universal Music Group, Warner Records, and Atlantic licensed recordings covered by a union agreement for a new artificial intelligence use without the required notice or payment to represented musicians. The pleaded claim concerns duties under a labour contract, leaving copyright ownership and the labels' authority over their masters outside the case. [37][38]

An August 19 notice dismissed only the wrongly named Warner Music Group Corp. The amended case continued against Warner Records, Atlantic, and Universal. Universal moved to dismiss and argued that the agreement provides no rate for artificial intelligence use, so its new-use clause supplies no payment duty. The union maintains that the clause covers the licences and requires both information and compensation. The contract question remained pending in the sources available for this update. [38][39][40][46]

Then the gate acquired teeth. On August 20, 2026, Music IP Holdings announced Udio and GRAI as the first licensees of a portfolio containing more than twenty-four issued or allowed patents and more than fifty pending applications. Its advertised scope reaches prompts, moderation, watermarking, authorization, distribution, and payment across music, video, images, text, and likeness uses. Music IP Holdings describes itself as the exclusive licensing entity for UMG's artificial intelligence and music technologies. [11][12]

The announcement proves the portfolio's advertised size, scope, UMG relationship, and first licensees. Patent validity, price, essentiality, and market coverage remain open. The visible structure already reaches farther than a catalogue. It offers patents for licensing across systems that handle prompts, authorization, tracking, distribution, and payment.

I admire the efficiency. Before the public could finish choosing heroes, courtroom enemies had become architects of the same market. Labels supply catalogues and organized permission. Technology companies supply models, interfaces, subscriptions, and controls over sharing and downloads. Patents and licences join those powers from prompt to payment. Artists supply the works, identities, and legitimacy that make the route valuable.

I enter catalogue, model, patent, and exit. The first three have owners. Whether the fourth exists, and on what terms, remains private.

## Several Leviathans rise

Danger creates demand for collective protection. A credible promise of protection recruits bodies, money, and legitimacy more efficiently than an exposed appetite for command. Protection authorizes an organized actor and gives it resources. That new capability alters the balance around it, so rivals expand because leaving survival in another's hands becomes intolerable. A settlement can end open conflict while dividing durable command among the powers that remain. Read together, Hobbes, Machiavelli, and Mearsheimer supply that single mechanism. [2][3][4]

Above my ledger hangs Abraham Bosse's 1651 frontispiece for Hobbes's *Leviathan*. A crowned sovereign holds sword and crozier while numerous smaller people compose its flesh. AI disputes now assemble several incomplete giants from the creators, performers, users, victims, and public invoked in their name. Their fear, labour, property, identity, and trust supply the bodies. Organized institutions acquire the hands. [36]

Infringement, piracy, unauthorized identity replication, opaque training, manipulation, and unsafe systems create demands for protection. Settlements, rules, and procedures convert those demands into capabilities and divide control among actors whose powers are unequal. I shall follow what survives each bargain. Who acquires capacity, who keeps the terms, and what can the person inside the giant still refuse?

Every claim in this field arrives Gorgonified. By the time an interest or injury reaches an institution powerful enough to act, it has become a factional claim, acquired an enforcement apparatus, and pointed toward an opposing apparatus. I keep the ledger because a worthy cause can build the same dependency as an unworthy one. Into it go money, licences, fees, secrecy, standards, jurisdiction, model access, enforcement, revocation, appeal, and exit.

That collision is Gorgonwars. The factions fight across the surface and reproduce the same field beneath it. Private firms seek control of infrastructure and permission. Lawyers aggregate claims and settlements. Courts exercise bounded public power over jurisdiction, evidence, and judgment. Legislatures create duties. Safety institutes build evaluation capacity and influence standards. Their powers, motives, and accountability differ. Gorgonification lies in the dependence created when protection travels through a gate one of them administers. Creators, performers, users, victims, and the public supply the terrain and bear the eventual terms.

## Culture enters two gates

The book litigation against Anthropic revealed three separate uses that public argument had compressed into training. Anthropic used books to train language models. It converted purchased print books into searchable digital copies after destroying the print copies. It also downloaded more than seven million pirated books for a permanent general library.

Judge William Alsup's June 23, 2025 order resolved two issues before trial. He ruled that the training copies and print conversions were fair use. He refused to give Anthropic the same pretrial victory for the pirated central library. The authors had filed no cross-motion seeking their own pretrial victory, so liability and damages remained for trial. The order itself entered no piracy judgment. [13]

The parties settled before trial. On July 20, 2026, Judge Araceli Martínez Olguín approved a $1.5 billion fund plus interest that could never return to Anthropic. The final Works List contained 482,460 works. As of April 16, 2026, claims covered 440,490 works, or at least 91.3 percent of that list. The estimated gross allocation reached approximately $3,000 per work before fees and costs. Anthropic agreed to destroy the original LibGen and PiLiMi files and copies originating from them, subject to preservation duties. The release covers past input conduct before August 25, 2025. Output claims and future conduct remain outside it. [14]

Objectors requested deletion of artificial intelligence models. The settlement supplied no model deletion remedy. Anthropic represented that neither the LibGen nor PiLiMi datasets, nor any portion of them, was included in the training corpus of any commercially released large language model. That statement is Anthropic's contractual representation. The court never adjudicated it as fact. The record therefore establishes neither commercial model contamination by those files nor judicially ordered model deletion.

The settlement class obtained a large fund and destruction of the covered LibGen and PiLiMi files and copies originating from them. The June 2025 pretrial ruling that the training use was fair use remained in place. The lawyers representing the class requested $187.5 million and received $101,561,111, with ten percent withheld pending distribution accounting. An appeal followed in August 2026. The public docket identifies the appellants and timing without stating the issue they intend to raise. Representation acquired its own price. [14][15]

One gate controls technical capability. A model company can absorb culture at scale and return that inheritance as a proprietary service. The other gate aggregates and administers claims to permission wherever permission is legally required or commercially negotiated. Publishers and collective licensors aggregate catalogues and negotiate licences. Class counsel assemble legal claims. Claims administrators process eligibility and payment. Courts approve settlements and fees. These functions can reinforce the permission gate without belonging to one actor.

The British government's March 2026 copyright and artificial intelligence assessment found that most reported licensing deals joined large rightsholders with large developers. It warned that universal licensing would impose substantial compliance costs and favour large rightsholders over individuals and smaller firms. A broad exception would favour developers and transfer value toward them. The government found considerable uncertainty across every option. [16]

The choice offered to the public therefore arrives predivided. Broad training strengthens the firms that own models and computing infrastructure. Universal licensing strengthens the organizations that own catalogues and administer permissions. A creator's practical options depend on whether a model firm, catalogue owner, licensing system, or class process recognizes the work and on the terms that institution sets.

The musicians' claim turns the engraving inward. Labels that invoked artists against artificial intelligence companies now face musicians invoking their labour agreement against the labels. The union offers protection through collective representation while asking the court to declare that the labels breached the agreement and to award damages on behalf of represented musicians. Every interest has arrived through an organized body.

I enter fund, deletion, training, and infrastructure. The settlement class obtained the first two. Anthropic retained the fair-use ruling and faced no model-deletion remedy. The settlement closed specified past input claims. Future use remains open.

## Identity enters the licence

A voice replica exposes the same engraving at the scale of one throat. The model produces the replica. A label, estate, platform, or licensing service may administer permission. The speaker enters the transaction as rights-holder, source material, and intended beneficiary. Those roles carry unequal power.

In *Lehrman v. Lovo*, voice actors Paul Lehrman and Linnea Sage allege that Fiverr buyers acting for Lovo promised narrow internal uses and paid $1,200 and $400. They allege that Lovo later used the recordings to build and sell synthetic voices called Kyle Snow and Sally Coleman. At the July 2025 motion-to-dismiss stage, Judge J. Paul Oetken allowed their contract claims, New York voice-misappropriation claims, Sage's claim over reuse of an actual recording, and a consumer-protection theory based on Lovo's alleged promise of full commercial rights to subscribers to proceed. [41]

The judge dismissed copyright claims aimed at generated clones because copyright protects fixed recordings and gives no copyright in a voice itself. He allowed the actors to replead a claim concerning copies allegedly made during training. They did, and Lovo moved again. The court had not decided that motion when Lovo filed for Chapter 7 bankruptcy in May 2026. On May 28, the court stayed the civil action and adjourned every deadline pending further order. The public sources reviewed through August 26 disclosed no later order. Liability, compensation, and class status remain unadjudicated. [41][42][43][44][45][47]

The United States Copyright Office recommended a new federal digital replica right in July 2024. Its proposal would cover all individuals and target the knowing distribution or public availability of highly realistic unauthorized replicas. It would permit licences with informed consent and safeguards concerning duration and minors. It would bar outright assignment, preserve a speech balancing test, and provide damages and injunctions. The Office recommended no comparable right in artistic style. [17]

Congress has yet to enact that general federal architecture. The 2026 NO FAKES proposals remain bills. [18][19]

A narrower federal regime is already in force. The TAKE IT DOWN Act criminalizes certain publication of nonconsensual intimate visual depictions. It also requires covered platforms to remove reported intimate images, including qualifying digital forgeries, and known identical copies within forty-eight hours. It creates no general right over voice, likeness, or artistic style. [35]

The Lovo order divides one voice among contract, state identity law, copyright in a fixed recording, and consumer law. The federal proposals would add another gate. Model owners control production and platform access. Labels, estates, platforms, and licensing services organize permission, enforcement, and payment. Warner's Suno announcement already places artist participation inside a private version of that structure.

Public language promises consent and compensation while contracts decide their reach. Specific consent can become a broad future licence. Payment can travel through an administrator whose accounting remains private. Under the Copyright Office proposal and Warner's announced opt-in structure, the person formally controls consent. Yet a performer can defeat the cloner and still become inventory for the institution licensed to protect the voice if that institution controls renewal, accounting, enforcement, and the practical price of refusal.

I enter consent, licence, payment, and refusal. The decisive entry is the next no. Whoever controls that refusal controls the next use.

## Inspection becomes a prize

California enacted AB 2013 in September 2024. Civil Code section 3111 requires covered developers to publish high-level documentation about datasets used to train generative artificial intelligence systems made available to Californians. The listed categories include sources, owners, licensing, personal information, processing, collection periods, first use, and synthetic data. The duty began on January 1, 2026 and applies before later public releases or substantial modifications. [20]

The statute asks for summaries. Model weights, source code, system architecture, every document, and every URL remain outside its listed categories. Section 3111 supplies a publication duty without its own inspection procedure or private cause of action.

xAI sued California's attorney general in December 2025. It alleged a taking of trade secrets, compelled speech, and unconstitutional vagueness. On March 4, 2026, the district court denied xAI's request to pause the law while the case proceeded. The judge found xAI's trade secret claims insufficiently specific at that stage. The First Amendment analysis treated the disclosure as likely commercial speech and left factual questions unresolved. The vagueness claim also remained open. [21][22]

xAI appealed. The district proceedings were stayed on March 23. As of August 26, xAI's optional reply brief was due September 4, no oral argument had occurred, and the Ninth Circuit had issued no decision. AB 2013 remained operative because no injunction had entered. [23][24]

The dispute concerns who may define necessary knowledge. California can compel a high-level summary. xAI can contest the boundary. The statute confines the public duty to its listed categories. The public receives the summary but no right to inspect the model under a rule whose constitutional limits remain unresolved.

Transparency has become another contested possession. Large developers can absorb documentation duties and litigate their boundaries. California gains a statutory model for compulsory high-level disclosure. Covered smaller developers face the same documentation rule with fewer resources to absorb compliance and litigation, although the relative burden remains unmeasured.

I enter a compulsory summary, undisclosed system details, and an empty inspection column for the user.

## Harm enters the court

Megan Garcia alleged that Character.AI's design contributed to the emotional dependence and February 2024 death of her son, Sewell Setzer III. Her complaint alleged product liability, negligence, wrongful death, consumer protection violations, and related claims against Character Technologies and connected defendants. [25]

The district court's May 2025 order accepted the pleaded allegations as true solely for the motions to dismiss. It permitted several claims concerning alleged app design defects to proceed. The court separated those design claims from liability for ideas or expressions inside the app. It also declined to classify all model output as protected speech on the limited record then available. The order established neither causation nor liability. [26]

The case generated limited evidence gathering to determine which defendants the court could reach, disputes over sealed material, and an unsuccessful request for an immediate appeal. The parties reported a settlement in January 2026. The court dismissed the case and later described it as settled, dismissed, and closed. The current docket marks it terminated. [27][28]

A family's loss entered a public system of pleading, jurisdiction, evidence gathering, sealing, and settlement. The plaintiff obtained bounded access to evidence. The court controlled jurisdiction and dismissal. The parties controlled settlement. Sewell's death and his family's loss are established while Character.AI's legal responsibility remains unadjudicated. The public record shows no payment, safety obligation, admission, or design change. I enter no judgment on liability and a bargain the public cannot read.

## Safety becomes an institution

The 2023 Bletchley Declaration named frontier artificial intelligence risks involving misuse, cybersecurity, biotechnology, disinformation, and loss of control. It called for testing, evaluation, transparency, accountability, public scientific capacity, and international cooperation. As a political declaration, it creates no binding inspection power, sanction, private right, or legal duty. [29]

The institutional machinery appears in the separate November 2 Chair's Statement. Governments recognized a growing role in external evaluation, promised public testing capacity, and proposed shared methods, standards, research, and information exchange. The United Kingdom announced an AI Safety Institute. [30]

The machinery continued. The 2024 Seoul process launched an international network of publicly backed safety institutes. By 2026 the network operated as the International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science. The United Kingdom's institute evaluates advanced models, receives priority access from participating developers, builds testing infrastructure, and informs policy. The 2026 International AI Safety Report assembled expert knowledge under the backing of more than thirty governments and international organizations. [31][32][33][34]

External evaluation answers a genuine information problem because companies possess models that outsiders cannot fully inspect. The answer has produced public laboratories, privileged access, expert authority, shared standards, and a permanent international network that converts scientific capacity into policy capacity.

Priority access from participating frontier developers gives public institutes the material from which shared tests, standards, and agendas are built. Companies gain recognized channels for submitting models and participating in testing practice. Smaller competitors enter terrain formed through relationships among states, experts, and frontier developers. Safety and innovation can each move decisions farther from the people living under them. The resulting network helps determine which dangers are measured, by whom, and with what tests.

I enter public testing capacity, privileged model access, emerging standards, and an empty public-inspection column.

## The final body still contains a person

The ledger preserves the unequal force of its instruments. Private settlements bind parties. Statutes impose duties. Agency reports recommend future rights. Political declarations organize cooperation. Courts administer claims. Their sources, force, and constraints differ. Each can still convert a legitimate interest into durable institutional capacity.

The hands are divided. Technology firms hold models, infrastructure, interfaces, and distribution. Catalogue owners and licensors hold organized permission. Lawyers assemble claims and settlements. Claims administrators process eligibility and payment. Courts control jurisdiction, procedure, sealing, and judgment. Legislatures impose disclosure duties. Safety institutes build evaluation capacity and influence standards. Each power can become a gate others must pass.

The benefits are real. Authors can be paid, identities protected, datasets disclosed, families given routes to evidence and possible remedies, and dangerous systems tested. Across these cases, protection demands a price in data, payment, disclosure, procedure, or accepted terms. That price becomes dependence on whoever operates the relevant contract, infrastructure, procedure, or enforcement machinery.

In every case here, protection against one organized power travels through another that retains control over at least one term of access, permission, evidence, or evaluation. The protection and the transferred dependence are both real. That is the evil versus evil structure.

The music contracts and Garcia settlement keep crucial terms outside public view. Evaluation institutions publish methods without surrendering models. Dataset summaries disclose categories while the underlying systems remain with their owners. These are different powers. Terms govern the bargain. Records govern what outsiders can verify from the official account. Whoever controls the terms sets the price of refusal. Whoever controls the record controls how much of that price enters public evidence. I enter both powers.

Inside the giant, the person lives under the terms. Outside it, the public sees only what enters the record. I close the ledger there.
