## Provenance rules used

- **User-authored statement** means a direct statement or ruling by the user in the conversation.
- **Pasted quotation** means material pasted into the conversation from another conversation, document, image, or site. Its original author remains unresolved unless the PDF itself identifies one.
- **Assistant synthesis** means interpretation, elaboration, proposed world-building, or generated research produced by an assistant. It cannot be converted into user-authored canon.
- **External citation** means a named outside work, author, site, or quotation. The PDF often gives incomplete bibliographic data, secondary quotations, or assistant-generated descriptions. These remain research leads until verified against the original work.
- Later ML* definitions control where they sharpen or supersede this older conversation. Early narrative formulations remain valuable source history and cannot override the current operational definitions by accident.
