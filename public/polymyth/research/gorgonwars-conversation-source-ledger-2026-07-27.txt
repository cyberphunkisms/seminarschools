FORTY-TWO-PRESSURE SOURCE/SYSTEM MATRIX
1. System · rank 1 Standpoint epistemology can turn position into epistemic rank and make ad hominem reasoning appear methodologically licensed.
2. System · rank 1 The personal is political can turn intimate friction into political jurisdiction and then into billable relation.
3. External pressure Feminism has no settled political subject. This asks whom the category represents.
4. System · diagnostic The two-root test asks whether a feminism retains standpoint rank, personal jurisdiction, or both.
5. System · self-audit The phrase gorgonified feminism is a qualifier-TWIST that protects an imagined clean remainder.
6. System · rank 3 Recursive intersectional correction can create a new excluded margin after every correction.
7. External pressure The essentialism bind asks how a thick category avoids exclusion and how a thin category retains political force.
8. External · system-tested; rejected as root objection Feminism operates as an umbrella over theories with incompatible surface premises. The system tests this objection by asking which accepted feminisms reject standpoint epistemology, “the personal is political,” or both. Surface incompatibility does not refute a shared root-selection mechanism when formations rejecting a root are moved to the boundary.
9. System · claim; external validation deferred Different material interests can divide coalitions. Polymyth’s stated refutation of the Hivemindidiom that inadequate material conditions simply explain falling births invokes higher fertility in poorer countries and Marx’s Theses on Feuerbach against vulgar materialism. The conversation set this battle aside. Demographic validation and controls remain deferred; this evidentiary debt does not erase the system claim. 17 (#note-17)
10. External · system-tested Factions identify different primary systems such as class, sex, race, colonialism, or the state. The two-root test asks whether the dispute still shares a deeper grammar.
11. External · system-tested Factions pursue conflicting goals such as equality, liberation, autonomy, abolition, recognition, or protection. Shared roots can survive divergent ends.
12. External · partly subsumed Factions make incompatible judgments about risk. Some conflicts also belong to broader progressivism rather than feminism alone.
13. System · rank 1 Protected-category routing can decide which injury enters institutional recognition before the facts are compared.
14. System · rank 1 Oppression can become epistemic rank and replace argument with credential.
15. System · rank 2 Mutual excommunication lets each faction declare the other an aberrant feminism.
16. System · rank 4 Conflict recoded as harm makes disagreement itself evidence of danger.
17. System · rank 3 Boundary policing keeps dissent visible as a hostile subtype and protects the centre from the counterexample.
18. External · rank 4 Trashing turns vague sisterhood into personal destruction. Inside standpoint rank, ad hominem can masquerade as epistemic method.
19. System · outcome Gorgonwars names two petrifying factions and the shared operation reproduced by their fight.
20. System · diagnostic The class question asks who materially benefits and why the person entered the exchange.
21. System · rank 1 Transactional solidarity converts care and coalition into cost-benefit accounting.
22. System · rank 3 Semantic elasticity lets a category expand while retaining moral authority earned by an earlier referent.
23. External · rejected as cause; no-pope Snakelogic Gallie’s contested-concept model can describe durable dispute. Saying feminism lacks a pope or final court merely restates that no decree settles the dispute. When offered as the cause of Gorgonwars, that restatement is Snakelogic because it routes away from the two-root selection mechanism that reproduces authority and jurisdiction. Gallie remains an external foil rather than the system’s explanation. 3 (#note-3)
24. External · rank 3 Structurelessness can hide power. Moving standards then shift the goalposts without a visible rule-maker.
25. System · rank 5 hypothesis Professionalization can reward a correction, then protect the jobs and procedures built from it. This is one proposed route into Snakelogic snowballing.
26. System · rank 5 Funding may select which grievances receive visibility and which diagnoses become inaudible. The mechanism must be tested institution by institution rather than assumed from the category.
27. System · rank 5 hypothesis Academic careers can depend on producing the next correction. The correction becomes inherited architecture.
28. System · rank 5 Platforms reward certainty, charge words, and accusation more reliably than reciprocal revision.
29. System · rank 5 Citation networks can become echo chambers when prestige substitutes for primary verification.
30. System · rank 2 One feminism blames white, carceral, neoliberal, liberal, radical, colonial, or exclusionary feminism and leaves the common machinery untried.
31. Unverified allegation The claim that UN Women regularly skews data remains an allegation. It needs a named dataset and a documented methodological discrepancy.
32. System · rank 5 Social platforms amplify factional accusation and convert internal war into engagement.
33. System · rank 4 The exact conversation example, “omg its 2025 and we still have to xyz,” generalizes as “it is [current year] and we still have to.” The Hivemindidiom turns chronology into proof and makes recurrence look like fresh emergency.
34. System · rejected objection The claim that plural foundations prevent a root diagnosis fails if the factions continue to share standpoint rank and personal jurisdiction.
35. System · rank 5 hypothesis Scarce attention, funding, status, and safety can intensify doctrinal competition.
36. System · rank 5 Organizations built to solve a continuing problem can acquire incentives to keep the problem administratively alive. This remains an institutional hypothesis to be tested case by case.
37. System · rank 3 Corrections harden into orthodoxies and produce the next excluded margin.
38. System · rank 5 Double Down Gorgonification and Snakelogic snowballing let credentialism, scientism, the replication crisis, diploma mills, Zionism, legalism, feminism, and psychologism grow into one another like Hydra heads.
39. External · rank 4 Under siege, an insider who names a flaw can be treated as helping the enemy. Coalition loyalty replaces correction.
40. System · rank 4 Insatiable insecurity and vicarious traumatizing keep protection demands expanding because second-hand pain supplies permanent moral fuel.
41. External claim rejected / System-user counterclaim; dated-corpus validation owed The external claim that each wave supplies genuinely new ideas lacked support and is rejected as an answer here. The user’s system counterclaim that four decades repeat the same Hivemindidioms is preserved rather than withdrawn. A dated corpus is still owed to validate it externally.
42. System · outcome Feminism performs unity while its factions remain at war. The performance conceals the mechanism that reproduces the war.
