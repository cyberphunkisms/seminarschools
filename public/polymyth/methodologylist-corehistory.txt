[both] Cave, fire, sun, many suns, and return in the older Mythology Integration Project layer
HISTORICAL SOURCE LAYER. The 249-page PDF develops a sequence of cave shadows, partial fire, forest, singular sun, Desert of many suns, continued turning, and return to the Cave.

OLDER FUNCTIONS. Shadows carry accepted stories and apparent reality. Fire carries partial logos and familiar containment. The forest opens broader reason, life, and awe. The singular sun carries an apparently complete illumination. The Desert introduces competing perspectives and the burden of plural realities. Continued turning becomes a discipline. Return converts insight into teaching, resistance, and responsibility.

CURRENT-RELATION RULE. This older layer remains archaeological substrate. Later direct user work controls the present sun, rays, Apeirogon, Philosopher's Stone, Point, and circle architecture. The old PDF contains no identification of the Philosopher's Stone with the sun and no rays-as-perspectives definition. That absence describes the older document and never cancels later direct development.

PROVENANCE. Moses, Jesus, Monkey King, Sufi turning, Homer Simpson, Nietzsche, and Plato appear as comparative leads requiring separate source verification.
