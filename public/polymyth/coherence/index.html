Polymyth Coherence

A reusable instrument for testing whether a work holds together within its own bounds, carries substantive relations across works or traditions, and supports claims beyond its frame.

Full Polymyth requires Deep audit across all three layers.

Internal is the absolute gate. Mezo or External evidence cannot repair an Internal failure.

Salience and truth-direction are reported only after coherence and never substitute for it.

Qualification sequence

The order is part of the method.

Bind the corpus before interpreting it. Test the released artifact before relations or outside claims. Report later judgments separately.

01

Bound scope

Name the released corpus, version, exclusions, and evidence grade.

02

Deep Internal

Test the artifact’s own commitments, organization, and contradictions.

03

Substantive Mezo

Record evidenced relations, permitted transformations, and load-bearing differences.

04

Supported External

Test at least one substantive claim that reaches beyond the work’s frame.

05

Separate outputs

Only then report salience and truth-direction without turning them into repairs.

Gate rule: a work does not qualify as fully Polymyth-coherent unless Internal, Mezo, and External each pass at Deep audit depth.

Three layers

One instrument, three distinct tests.

Layer 1

Internal

The bounded released artifact must be contradiction-free and substantively organized on its own terms. Peripheral and production evidence may count when it bears on the work.

An unresolved Internal failure blocks qualification. Neither reception nor outside truth can repair it.

Layer 2

Mezo

At least one substantive relation must be evidenced and typed. The relation records what is inherited, what may be transformed, and which differences carry structural weight.

Resemblance alone is not enough. The relation must do explanatory work.

Layer 3

External

At least one substantive claim outside the work’s frame must be identified and supported. A work need not be realist to make an External claim.

An unsupported outside claim cannot borrow strength from Internal elegance or Mezo reach.

File boundary

The instrument stays stable across cases.

The method and its applications have different jobs. Keeping them separate prevents one convenient verdict from rewriting the test.

Canonical instrument

This star file owns the complete Internal, Mezo, and External method, evidence rules, audit gates, ledgers, red-team checks, regression tests, and workbook.

Application files

Individual media audits remain separate application files and cannot alter the instrument. Films, books, games, traditions, and other cases keep their own findings and evidence.

Post-coherence outputs: salience and truth-direction remain separate judgments. They do not repair a failed layer and do not become a substitute coherence score.

Canonical workbook

Run the complete audit.

The workbook carries the operational rulebook, case ledgers, source map, coverage checks, repair log, red-team QA, and regression evidence.
