Automation boundary: AI may extract locators, propose ledger entries, check completeness, and flag candidates. Automated output alone cannot establish a repair ruling, a Deep verdict, or Full qualification.

Three layers

The criteria do not change with the case.

Layer 1

Internal

The bounded released artifact must be contradiction-free and substantively organized on its own terms. Peripheral and production evidence may count when it bears on the work.

An unresolved Internal failure blocks qualification. Neither reception nor outside truth can repair it.

Layer 2

Mezo

At least one substantive relation must be evidenced and typed. The relation records what is inherited, what may be transformed, and which differences carry structural weight.

Resemblance alone is not enough. The relation must do explanatory work.

Layer 3

External

At least one substantive claim outside the work’s frame must be identified and supported. A work need not be realist to make an External claim.

An unsupported outside claim cannot borrow strength from Internal elegance or Mezo reach.

File boundary

Criteria and case results have different homes.

Keeping them separate prevents a convenient finding from rewriting the test and prevents a prior result from becoming an answer key for a new case.

Canonical instrument

Owns the rules, protocol, evidence standards, blank ledger schemas, formulas, QA definitions, source map, and regression tests. TEMPLATE-001 is an uncompleted scaffold.

Application files

Own the populated case inputs, evidence, review, judgments, blocker codes, and results. They may cite an instrument version but cannot alter its criteria.

Full qualification: protocol fit, primary evidence, Deep depth, workbook integrity, all forty QA checks, a qualified conclusion, independent review, and passing Internal, Mezo, and External results are all required. Internal is the absolute gate. Salience, truth-direction, reception, and second-order meaning cannot repair a first-order failure.
