How BB is played

  1. Choose the anchor text. A story, poem, article, film, image, song, game, or historical document becomes the world.
  2. Make wormcards. Each player builds a character who can cross into that world.
  3. Give the wormcards to the Dimensional Master. The human guide and AI translate them into play.
  4. Turn assignments into quests. There are no dice. Reading, writing, research, revision, and discussion drive the consequences.
  5. Let time matter. Record what changed: work, discoveries, open problems, and next steps.

Made first for voluntary seminar play

Use BB in a reading group, club, tutoring session, family, or seminar. Schools can use the same text-world logic for required work.
