Classroom fit

Students write as Roger, 20 years after the encounter, composing a thank-you and apology letter to Mrs. Jones. Letter quality determines writing quality assessment. Stronger writing earns a higher level, which opens richer possibilities in the game. Parallel assignment: poorly written letter from the kids who locked Margot in the closet (Bradbury, All Summer in a Day). Ontario OSSD: Writing strand D1 Developing Ideas, D2 Creating Texts. IB: Paper 1 guided textual analysis, HL essay.
