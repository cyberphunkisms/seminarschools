Classroom fit

Post-reading lesson. Opens with discussion about whether stealing is ever acceptable. Students identify theme/central idea and write a thank-you note to one of the characters. Aligned to CCSS RL.9-10.2 (determine theme, analyze development). Free preview; full access requires subscription.
