À propos de cette fiche

The organizer confirms a 2027 prize and says its deadline will be announced in September 2026. Current K to 12 students worldwide, including home-study students, may enter an essay of approximately 1,000 words. The prize is US$500 plus a one-year World History Association membership, and membership is not required to enter. The September 1 date marks the announced update window.
