Corrections and new information

Help keep the directory useful.

Found an outdated link, missing project, or incorrect detail? Send the change and the best source you have. An editor checks it before publication, and older book information is never silently replaced.

01

Suggest a project

Name a library, archive, repository, portal, journal, collaboratory, preservation network, civic information commons, or shared knowledge infrastructure.

02

Correct a record

Fix a name, description, project type, responsible organization, date, current status, or source.

03

Report a broken link

Send the record ID, failing URL, and any current official destination, successor, or archive you can establish.

04

Suggest a connection

Connect a project to a responsible organization, host, partner, member network, standard, earlier project, successor, or archive.

05

Share official project information

If you represent a listed project, provide its official description, current website, responsible organization, access information, and direct source links.

06

Add an archive or preservation link

Identify an official archive, repository, mirror, successor collection, or other lasting way to reach a moved or discontinued project.

After you send it

What happens to a suggested change?

The directory currently accepts changes by email. Published corrections are intended to keep a visible revision history.

01Send

A reader or project representative describes the change and includes supporting links.

02Check

An editor confirms what the sources say and when they were published or reviewed.

03Resolve

Duplicate records, name changes, conflicts, and the best place for the information are reviewed.

04Publish

The record is updated with its sources, check date, and a note about what changed.
