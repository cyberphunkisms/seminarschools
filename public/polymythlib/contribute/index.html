What happens to a proposal?

A visible proposal history is the intended governance record. The initial release uses structured email while the directory remains curator-led.

01Submit

A person or project representative provides a claim and links.

02Check evidence

Direct official sources, dates, and the precise claim are separated.

03Editor review

Duplicates, lineage, scope, vocabulary, and conflicts are resolved.

04Publish

The record, evidence, verification date, and revision note are released.
