## Front-facing and anti-yap audit

Visitor-facing chrome was revised across Saul’s CV, Polymyth, Polymythcal,
About, Polymyth Commons, and Polymythlib. Internal builder language, AI
self-instructions, raw generation labels, and long provenance notes were
replaced with concise public wording. Saul’s experience filters now speak to a
visitor choosing relevant work, and the calendar describes public listings and
updates rather than its internal generation process.

The anti-yap boundary is selective. Articles, research entries, archive bodies,
methodology editions, and substantive teacher material were not shortened.
The verifier blocks the exact interface regressions without treating ordinary
words such as “source” or “AI” as universal errors.
